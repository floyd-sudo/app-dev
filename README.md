# My Favorite Movie

## The Perks of Being a Wallflower

**Directed by:** *Stephen Chbosky*  
**Genre:** Drama / Coming-of-Age  
**Release Year:** 2012  

---

###  About the Movie
*The Perks of Being a Wallflower* tells the story of **Charlie**, a shy high school student who learns about friendship, love, and growing up.

> “We accept the love we think we deserve.”

---

###  Why I Like It
- The story feels real and emotional.  
- The characters are relatable.  
- It teaches about self-acceptance and hope.
